# Data Sources Overview

## CRM
- **deals**: Number of closed deals.
- **pipeline**: Total potential value in pipeline.
- **win_rate**: Ratio of won to total opportunities.
- **churn**: Customer churn rate.

## Finance
- **mrr**: Monthly recurring revenue.
- **burn**: Net cash burn for the period.
- **ar_aging**: Days outstanding on accounts receivable.

## Support
- **tickets**: Total support tickets logged.
- **resolution_time**: Avg time (hours) to close a ticket.
- **csat**: Customer satisfaction score (1‑5).

## Projects
- **milestones**: Completed project milestones.
- **completion_pct**: Overall project completion percentage.

## Custom
- **active_users**: Number of active platform users.
- **feature_adoption**: Percentage of users adopting the latest feature.
